Straight answers

The questions every busy parent asks.

I genuinely have no time. How much does this need?+

20 to 30 minutes a day for training, and a way of eating that fits the meals you already cook. The whole point of YPT90 is that it is engineered for people with full days. If you can find 30 minutes, the plan does the rest.

Do I need a gym?+

No. Your plan is built for whatever you have, whether that is a full gym, a few dumbbells at home or just your body weight. We design around your access, not against it.

I am vegetarian and eat normal Indian food. Will this work?+

Yes. Plans are built around Indian food, your kitchen and your preferences, vegetarian or not. Eating out and family meals are planned in, not banned.

I am in my 50s. Is it too late?+

No. A large share of the people I coach are in their 40s and 50s. Your metabolism has not given up. It has been running on a plan that was never built for it.
